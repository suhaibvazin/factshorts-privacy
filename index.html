Fact-ory

Interesting facts, served fresh — in under a minute.

Fact-ory is a YouTube Shorts channel that turns trending news, future tech and forgotten history into short, fun, well-researched fact videos, presented by an animated host.

About this app

The "Fact-ory" Google app is a personal publishing tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads and schedules the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's YouTube account or data.
